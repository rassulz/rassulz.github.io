🤖 My academic interests focus on **industrial manipulators** and **autonomous mobile robots**, particularly their applications in automation and intelligent systems, with a strong emphasis on **artificial intelligence** and **reinforcement learning**.

### 🏭 Automation and Robotics Engineer — **Hyundai Trans Kazakhstan**

* **Car Spot Welding:** Participated in the integration of the **Hyundai Accent body model** into robotic welding cells, gaining hands-on experience in workflow setup, configuration, and **trajectory path optimization** for automated production.

* **Autonomous Guided Vehicles (AGVs):** Studied Hyundai’s AGV systems used for **intralogistics and material transport**. Explored **PLC communication protocols** for AGV navigation, coordination, and precise body transfer across assembly lines.

* **PLC Programming:** Designed **ladder logic** in **Siemens TIA Portal (S7-300)** to control a conveyor system, implementing start/stop commands, direction switching, emergency stop, and **timer-based sequences**.

* **HMI Development:** Developed operator interfaces in **XP Builder (LS Electronics)** for real-time conveyor visualization and control, including functional buttons (Start, Reset, Direction, E-Stop) and sensor status indicators.

* **Sensor Integration:** Installed and calibrated **inductive proximity sensors**, enabling automated start, delay, and stop sequences synchronized with conveyor operation.

* **Electrical Assembly:** Assembled and wired a complete **control cabinet** with circuit breakers, relays, power supplies, timers, and signaling devices, ensuring safe, reliable, and compliant system operation.
