Conference Publication

Distributed Control Framework of Multirobot System Using Automation and Control Applications

Rassul Zeinulla, Almat Abdimalik, Ayan Rakhmetkali, Collins Masimba, and Timur Samigulin.

Accepted conference paper, 2026 IEEE 20th International Conference on Control and Automation (ICCA), Almaty, Kazakhstan, June 2026.

The paper presents a distributed control architecture for a laboratory-validated multirobot painting system. It combines Siemens SIMATIC S7-1500 PLC sequencing and safety interlocks with MATLAB/Simulink supervisory control, OPC UA communication, two RoArm-M2-S manipulators, and a KUKA KR10 industrial robot.
